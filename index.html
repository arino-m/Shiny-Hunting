Shiny and Spawn Mechanics

Your game will store up to 10 shinies across Lumiose City that have previously spawned in. These shinies can be left and revisited at any time to catch. Any additional shinies that spawn in past 10 will cause the oldest shiny to despawn. If a shiny is KOed or runs away, it will NOT respawn.

The spawn radius of Pokemon is 50 meters. This means that spawn locations that are within 50 meters will always have their respective Pokemon unless it has been recently KOed. The despawn radius of Pokemon is 70 meters. This allows for Pokemon that have already been spawned in to stay given that you are not more than 70 meters away. Using a Pin is the best way to measure distances and can be used as a guide when resetting spawns.

  • Shiny Locked Pokémon - Gift Pokémon, In-game trade Pokémon, Scripted encounters, Rogue Mega Pokémon, Legendaries/mythicals.
    • Exceptions - Base - Fossil Pokémon; DLC - Latias, Latios, Cobalion, Terrakion, Virizion.
  • The DLC Legendaries listed above cannot be alpha but are affected by the shiny charm and sparkling power.
  • Fossil Pokémon obtained via reviving fossils cannot have their shiny or alpha odds increased (1/4096 Shiny; 1% Alpha).

Hyperspace Lumiose

Hyperspace Lumiose is the dimension that the player can access from the DLC. This area has different spawn mechanics than the overworld, however, these spawns generally follow certain patterns. Looking at these patterns is usful in optimizing shiny hunts.

  • Groupings are the groups of Pokemon that spawn in a Hyperspace Wild Zone. Pokemon will always spawn in groups of 2 - 6 depending on the size of the wild zone. Small wild zones generally have 4 groups containing 4 Pokemon each, however, some small wild zones have 2 groups of 6 and 1 group of 4 or many groups of 2 and 4. Optimally, you want the Placement of these groups close to the starting point (close enough to fly reset them) and you want the specific Pokemon you are hunting in 1 or more of those groups. Medium and Large wild zones have much more variance in how and where groups spawn. Generally, Pokemon spawn in groups of 6 and can also spawn on top of the roofs. You want these groupings to be close to either horizontal holovators or the fly location. If positioned correctly, some groups can be hunted via horizontal holovator resets which is the most consistent and effective way to hunt in Hyperspace Lumiose. Note that small wild zones with only 3 total groups will always have the same predetermined species in each group and this cannot be changed. Also note that not every medium or large Hyperspace Wild Zone will have an optimal holovator reset and some will be inaccessible to fly resets if a group spawns too close to the portal.

This image shows a Hyperspace wild zone where Flabebe and Floette cannot be reset via fly resets. Alpha Flabebe or Floette will engage the player and prevent them from flying.

These images show a group of Spritzee and Aromatisse that can be hunted via a horizontal holovator. The holovator brings the player into the spawn radius of the Pokemon then out of the despawn radius causing the group to spawn and despawn over and over. These resets are very fast and, if done optimally, can cause your calorie count to barely go down every reset.

  • Wild Zone Tiers refer to the amount of stars a Wild Zone has. This ranges from 1 to 5, however, 5 star zones can only appear from Special Scans. Certain Pokemon require certain tiers to appear. The full list of Pokemon and their spawn conditions can be found here. The higher the wild zone tier, the faster your calories are depleted as shown here. To most effectively utilize your donuts, it is better to hunt Pokemon in the lowest tier wild zone they appear in. This is especially true if you wish to hunt a lot of the same Pokemon since the longer you are in a zone, the more shinies you can get.
